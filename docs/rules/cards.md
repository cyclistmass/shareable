# Every card

Each card shows its name and one line of text in the game. That line, word for word, is quoted here under the name;
below it are the full rules as the app plays them. Copies are per game.

## Fortune cards (base game)

25 cards. Bought for 1 Fleece, 1 Grain and 1 Stone. Play at most one per turn, never on the turn you bought it.

### Sentinel (14)
> Move the bandit and take a card from a player next to it. Three or more win the Strongest Watch.

Before or after you roll. Move the Bandit to another tile, then take a random card from one player with a building on
it. Your third Sentinel played wins the Strongest Watch (2 points), unless someone has played more.

### Windfall (2)
> Pick 2 resources from the bank, alike or different.

You may take just one only if the bank can't give you any two.

### Corner the Market (2)
> Name a resource: everyone gives you all of theirs.

Every other player hands you every card they hold of that resource.

### Trailblazers (2)
> Build 2 roads for free.

The roads go down at once, before anything else. If you have only one road left, or room for only one, you get one.

### Landmark (5)
> Worth 1 point (kept hidden until the end).

Never played. It counts toward your points from the moment it's in your hand.

## Progress cards (Age of Chivalry)

54 cards in three decks of 18. They are drawn through the gates on the event die and as a reward for beating the
Raiders, never bought. You may hold 4 (point cards don't count). Play any number on your turn after rolling, except the
Transmuter, which is played before rolling.

### Academy (Parchment)

#### Transmuter (2)
> Before you roll: choose what the two number dice show.

Instead of rolling, set the red and ivory dice to any numbers. The event die is still rolled. The roll then plays out
as usual, gates included.

#### Hoist (2)
> Improve a town upgrade (Guilds, Academy or Crown) for one commodity less.

Make one improvement at once, in any track, paying one commodity fewer than its level (level 1 is free). Capitals are
won as usual.

#### Master Mason (1)
> Build Ramparts on one of your towns for free.

One of your Towns without Ramparts gets them. You need Ramparts left in your supply.

#### Tinkerer (2)
> Two tiles trade numbers; 2, 12, 6 and 8 never move.

Pick two tiles with different numbers, neither one a 2, 12, 6 or 8. Their numbers swap places for the rest of the game.

#### Waterworks (2)
> Each Grainfield touching your homesteads or towns pays you 2 Grain.

Counted per tile, not per building, and only as far as the bank has Grain.

#### Healer (2)
> Turn a homestead into a town for 2 Stone and 1 Grain.

You need a Town left in your supply.

#### Deep Shafts (2)
> Each Quarry touching your homesteads or towns pays you 2 Stone.

Counted per tile, not per building, and only as far as the bank has Stone.

#### Printing Press (1)
> Worth 1 point; played at once.

Shown the moment it's drawn and kept for good. It doesn't count toward your hand of 4.

#### Pathfinders (2)
> Build 2 roads for free.

Lay them at once or say Done. One road if that's all you have left.

#### Forge (2)
> Up to 2 of your knights go up a strength, free.

The usual rules apply: mighty needs Crown level 3, a free knight of the higher strength, and each knight goes up only
once a turn. Say Done to keep the second.

### Guilds (Fabric)

#### Harbour Charter (2)
> Offer each other player a resource; each must give you a commodity back.

Once for each player holding a commodity: hand them one resource of your choice, and they give you one commodity of
theirs. Stop whenever you like. You need a resource to give.

#### Grand Factor (2)
> Pick a player ahead of you on points: see their hand and take 2 cards.

Resources or commodities, your choice. Fewer if they hold fewer.

#### Peddler (6)
> Put the Trader on a tile next to you: trade its resource 2 for 1, and hold 1 point while it's yours.

There is one Trader. It goes on a land tile, not the desert, touching your Homestead or Town. Whoever plays the next
Peddler takes it, with the point.

#### Swift Hulls (2)
> This turn, trade one card type with the bank 2 for 1.

Name a resource or a commodity. Two Swift Hulls can cover two different types.

#### Corner the Field (4)
> Name a resource: each other player hands you 2 of it, or all they have.

#### Corner the Wares (2)
> Name a commodity: each other player hands you 1 of it, if they have one.

### Crown (Silver)

#### Abbot (2)
> Move the bandit and take a random card from each player on that tile.

Only once the Raiders have landed and the Bandit is loose. Move it to another tile, then take a random card from each
other player with a building there who holds one.

#### Charter of Rights (1)
> Worth 1 point; played at once.

Shown the moment it's drawn and kept for good. It doesn't count toward your hand of 4.

#### Turncoat (2)
> A player takes one of their knights off; you may set down one of yours of that strength.

Pick a player with a knight on the board; they choose which knight goes back to their supply. If you have a knight of
that strength in your supply and a free corner on your roads, you may put it there, supplied if theirs was.

#### Envoy (2)
> Lift any road with a loose end; if it was yours, you may lay it again.

A loose end is one with no other road or building of that road's owner. If the road was yours, you may lay one road
free, straight away.

#### Plot (2)
> Knock an enemy knight off one of your roads.

Any strength, on a corner touching one of your roads. Its owner moves it along their own roads to an empty corner, or
back to their supply if there's nowhere to go.

#### Wrecker (2)
> Everyone level with you on points or ahead throws away half their cards.

Rounded down, resources and commodities together. They choose which.

#### Informer (3)
> Look at a player's progress cards and take one.

Pick a player who holds progress cards, look at them all, and keep one.

#### Marshal (2)
> Supply all your knights for free.

Knights supplied this way can't act this turn.

#### Feast (2)
> Everyone ahead of you on points hands you 2 cards of their choosing.

Resources or commodities. All they have, if that's fewer than 2.

## Defender of the Realm (Age of Chivalry, 6)

Not drawn from a deck. When the knights beat the Raiders, the one player who gave the most strength takes a Defender of the Realm card:
1 point, kept for good. The turn report names who won it. If players tie, or the cards are gone, each of them draws a progress card instead.
